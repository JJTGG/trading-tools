# Trading Tools Roadmap

Trading Tools is being developed as a decision-support workspace for traders.

The roadmap describes product direction rather than fixed deadlines. Priorities may change as the product is built, tested, and used.

## Current Focus

The current priority is strengthening the foundation that connects individual tools into a coherent trading workflow.

- Reusable trade context across tools
- Stronger workflow continuity between market data, calculations, and analysis
- Trade planning
- Risk management
- Trade records and review
- Better use of the existing workspace and persistent user data
- Continued refinement of the Market Terminal and market context

The goal is to make Trading Tools feel like one connected workspace rather than a collection of separate calculators.

## Near Term

The next stage of development is focused on expanding the core decision-support workflow.

- More capable trade-planning workflows
- Improved analysis tools
- Better connections between market context and trading calculations
- Trade journaling and structured records
- Review workflows for completed trades
- More useful workspace-level persistence
- Improvements to usability, reliability, and product consistency

## Later

Once the core workflow is established, Trading Tools can expand into deeper analysis and research capabilities.

- Advanced trade analytics
- Portfolio and performance analysis
- Historical trade analysis
- Strategy simulation
- Backtesting
- More advanced research workflows
- Additional market data capabilities
- AI-assisted analysis where it provides genuine decision-support value

These areas are intentionally not treated as immediate commitments.

## Future Integrations

### Trading Signal Dropper (TDS)

TDS is a separate project and is not currently integrated into Trading Tools.

The long-term direction is for TDS and Trading Tools to complement each other rather than become one indistinguishable system.

The intended relationship is:

**TDS identifies or delivers a trading opportunity → Trading Tools provides the context, analysis, planning, risk management, decision, and record around it.**

A future integration may allow a signal or opportunity from TDS to become a starting point for a structured trade workflow inside Trading Tools.

This integration is intentionally deferred until the underlying Trading Tools workflow is mature enough for the connection to provide meaningful value.

## Ideas / Exploration

These are areas that may be explored as the product develops. They are not commitments.

- Additional decision-support tools
- More advanced market research
- Strategy and setup analysis
- Deeper performance insights
- Expanded simulation capabilities
- Additional integrations with useful trading systems
- AI-assisted workflows that reduce repetitive analysis without replacing the trader's decision

## Product Boundary

Trading Tools is a decision-support product.

It is not intended to become a broker, exchange, custody platform, or trade-execution system.

The roadmap should continue to prioritize tools that improve the quality, consistency, and traceability of a trader's decision-making process.

## Roadmap Philosophy

The roadmap is intentionally flexible.

Features should be added because they solve real problems in the trading workflow, not simply because they are technically possible.

Priorities can change when testing, user feedback, or actual product usage provides better evidence about what Trading Tools should become.
