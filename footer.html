Product boundary

Decision support. Not trade execution.

Trading Tools provides calculators, market information, research references, planning support, and workspace functionality.

It does not execute trades, place orders, hold funds, custody assets, or act as a broker. Information and calculations are provided for decision support and do not constitute financial advice.
