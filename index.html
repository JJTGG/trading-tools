THE IDEA

Trading is easier to reason about when the pieces stay connected.

Entry, stop, target, position size, leverage, fees, market conditions, and the eventual outcome are not isolated numbers. They are parts of the same decision.

Trading Tools is built around that connection. Calculate one thing, carry the context forward, and keep the work you already started.
